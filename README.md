# Metricgrain

**Snowflake cost, data modeling and AI analytics for teams that live in the warehouse.**

<br clear="left">

We help data teams spend less on Snowflake and get more trustworthy answers out of it.

## What we do

- **Snowflake cost audits.** Every fix priced in dollars per year, ranked by payback, quick wins applied.
- **Semantic layers and dimensional models.** dbt Semantic Layer on top of Kimball models, so dashboards and AI tools agree on every number.
- **AI analytics.** Natural-language analytics and MCP servers built on governed metrics, with evaluation sets that measure answer accuracy.

## Free tools

- [**snowflake-cost-scan**](snowflake-cost-scan/): one read-only SQL script that shows where your Snowflake money goes and how much you could save. About 2 minutes to run.

## Track record

- $1.3M in cumulative Snowflake savings at a $2B SaaS unicorn
- $850K a year saved on Databricks at a $1B unicorn
- Customer-facing AI analytics: 92% answer accuracy across 300+ governed metrics
